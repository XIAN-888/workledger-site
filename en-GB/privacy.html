Hourlume privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Hourlume helps you record client work, prepare statements and track payments. Your work records stay on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your client names, contact details, notes, work hours, amounts or exported documents.

Records on your device

The app saves clients, work, statements, payments, receipt allocations and local recovery copies in its own storage. Saved receipts include a reduced-size image copy, recognised text and the fields you confirmed. Personal and sample workspaces are separate. There is no app account, advertising, tracking, usage analytics or third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal data.

Receipt recognition and confirmation

When you choose a receipt image, Apple Vision performs text recognition on your device. The app does not upload the image or recognised text to us, Apple recognition servers or another recognition-service server. Recognition proposes fields for your review; it does not certify authenticity or that money reached a bank account.

Saving the confirmed payment stores the reduced-size receipt copy, recognised text, confirmed amount/currency/date and allocation links in your local ledger. Cancelling the review without saving adds no receipt evidence to the ledger. Reversing a recorded receipt keeps its evidence and correction history. Your original Photos or Files item is managed separately.

Files you choose to save or share

PDF, CSV and JSON files can contain your client and work information. You choose where to save or share them through Apple’s Files and sharing panels. A destination you choose may be a cloud provider or another app with its own privacy practices. Your device-backup settings may also include app data in Apple’s device backups.

There is no automatic app cloud sync or cross-device sync. Moving records between iPhone and iPad requires a JSON backup that you transfer and restore yourself. Restoration replaces the selected workspace; it does not merge devices.

Complete JSON backups embed the saved receipt copies, recognised text and confirmed payment fields. A backup or export you choose to send can therefore disclose that information to its destination; the app does not automatically send it to us.

Purchases through Apple

Apple processes payments and purchase restoration. The app uses StoreKit to verify annual-subscription or lifetime-purchase access. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple’s processing is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Manage or cancel an annual subscription in your Apple Account settings. A lifetime purchase does not automatically cancel an existing annual subscription.

Local export-access records

To remember completed free exports and statements already unlocked, the app stores randomly assigned statement identifiers (UUIDs) and their free or Pro unlock type in the device-only iOS Keychain. These records do not contain client names or statement amounts and are not sent to us. They also prevent restoring an older backup on that device from resetting the three-statement free allowance.

JSON backups preserve the unlock status attached to each included statement. Restoring such a backup on another device keeps those exact statements unlocked. This is a user-directed file transfer, not automatic Keychain or app sync.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or archive clients, restore recently deleted work, reverse payment records and export a JSON backup. Removing the app deletes its app-container records; local Keychain export-access identifiers may remain. Exported files remain wherever you saved or shared them. Manage those copies in the destination you chose.
