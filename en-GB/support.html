Hourlume support

A simple path from recorded work to a clear statement. Hourlume is for iPhone and iPad; there is no Mac version.

Start with a client

Add a client and an hourly rate. Start a timer or enter completed work manually. Each saved work entry keeps its recorded rate and currency when you change a client’s defaults later.

Prepare and share a statement

Choose unbilled work for one client and one currency. Preview the PDF, then save or share a PDF or CSV using Apple’s sharing panel. The app does not send statements automatically.

Work included in a statement is locked. If a statement has no recorded payments, you can void it to release the work for corrections. A statement documents your work; it is not a tax invoice or a payment-processing service.

Record money you have received

Choose a receipt image from Photos or Files. Apple Vision proposes text, amount, currency and date locally. Read the source, correct the fields and explicitly confirm that you actually received the money. OCR does not verify authenticity, a bank transfer or settlement. Missing, negative or ambiguous values may require manual entry.

Review the allocation to the same client and currency, oldest unpaid statement first, before saving it as one receipt. Reversing one allocation reverses the whole receipt while retaining its history; it does not issue a refund. An identical source image cannot be recorded twice, and possible duplicates need your review. Cancelling before saving adds no receipt evidence to your ledger.

Explain a period balance

Open Clients, select a client, then tap Client reconciliation (the page title is Reconciliation). Choose a currency and date period. Review the source statements, received and reversed amounts, dated voids, and opening and closing unpaid balances. Other currencies and unbilled work remain separate. If an older void has no recorded date, the app identifies unavailable historical balances rather than inventing them.

Three free statements

Client management, work tracking and previews are free. Your first three different formal statements can be exported for free. A statement counts only after a successful save or share; previewing or cancelling the sharing panel does not use an allowance.

Once a statement has been unlocked by a completed free or Pro export, you can export that same statement again without another allowance, even after your annual subscription ends. PDF and CSV of the same statement share the same unlock. Sample statements and JSON backup and restore are always free.

Reconciliation previews are free. A combined PDF or CSV can be shared for sample data, with active Pro, or when every included statement already has permanent export access. It creates no new quota unit and unlocks no unexported statement. If access is missing, use the original statement export or Pro flow first; the original three-statement rules still apply.

Annual or lifetime Pro

Pro unlocks exports of additional new statements. Choose an annual auto-renewing subscription or a one-off lifetime purchase. The price and billing details shown in the app and Apple’s confirmation apply to your storefront.

To restore a purchase, open Settings → Pro → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel an annual subscription in your Apple Account’s subscription settings. Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel an existing annual subscription; cancel it separately if you no longer want it to renew. Apple handles payments and restoration. The Apple standard EULA applies.

Back up or move to another device

There is no automatic cloud or cross-device sync. To move records between your iPhone and iPad, use a JSON backup yourself. Restoring replaces the selected workspace, rather than merging it. Language and statement-sender settings are not included in the backup.

A complete JSON backup includes saved reduced-size receipt images, recognised text, confirmed fields and receipt allocations as well as the workspace records. Treat it as private financial information and check the receipt attachments after restoring.

The complete encoded backup is limited to 256 MB and each saved receipt image to 8 MB. An over-limit error leaves existing records intact; contact support if needed. Keep a backup before upgrading. An older app cannot safely open newly upgraded records; returning to it requires restoring your pre-upgrade backup.

  1. On the source device, open Settings → Save backup to Files and choose a destination.
  2. Transfer that JSON file to the destination device, for example through Files or AirDrop.
  3. Save a backup of any records you want to keep on the destination device. Open Settings → Restore backup, review the contents and confirm the replacement.
  4. Check your clients, work, statements and payments after restoring. Sample and personal workspaces remain separate; restore the backup to the matching workspace.

Choose your language

The app follows your system language by default. In Settings you can choose U.S. English, U.K. English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, German, Korean or Spanish. This website opens in U.S. English by default; use its language links to switch.
